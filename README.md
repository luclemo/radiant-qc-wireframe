# Radiant QC tab · clickable wireframe

A clickable prototype of the redesigned QC tab on the Radiant case page.

**Try it:** https://luclemo.github.io/radiant-qc-wireframe/

## What you can do

- Switch between the three views: Overview, Gene coverage and Metric details.
- Use the **Prototype controls** panel (bottom right) to change the case:
  - Language: English or French
  - Identity check: pass or fail
  - Family: solo, trio, family of 4, or mother + sibling
  - Fetal analysis: on or off
- Press **H** to hide or show the controls.

## Good to know

- This is a wireframe. The data is demo data, and the design is not final.
- Thresholds come from an earlier tool and are not yet confirmed by the lab.
- The panel of prototype controls is not part of the design.
